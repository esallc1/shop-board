How to ask us to delete your information

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you in any of these ways:

  • Send "Delete my data" to our Facebook Page or Instagram account in a message.
  • Email service@leetransmissionauto.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  • Call us at (239) 491-2809, Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm.

Please include your name and the phone number or Facebook/Instagram name you used to contact us, so we can find your records.

What happens next

We will confirm that we received your request and complete it within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your Facebook/Instagram messages to us and the ID number Meta gave us for your conversation.
  • Your answers to our lead forms.
  • Your contact details and notes, if you never had work done at our shop.

What we may have to keep

If we repaired your vehicle, we must keep invoices, payment records and warranty records for as long as tax law and warranty obligations require. We keep only what is needed for that and delete the rest.
