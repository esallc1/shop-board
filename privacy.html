What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle your information when you contact our shop — by phone, Facebook Messenger, Instagram direct message, or a Facebook lead form — and when we service your vehicle.

What we collect

  • Your name and the public profile name on your Facebook or Instagram account.
  • The ID number Meta gives us for your conversation with our Page (it only works with our Page).
  • The messages you send us and our replies, including automatic replies.
  • Information you choose to give us: phone number, email, and vehicle details (year, make, model, VIN, license plate, mileage, the problem you are having).
  • Answers you submit on our Facebook lead forms.
  • For calls to our business lines: your phone number, the date and time, and a recording of the call. Calls may be recorded.
  • Service records: estimates, repair orders, invoices and payment records.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, schedule appointments and follow up with you.
  • To prepare estimates, repair your vehicle, invoice you and handle warranty work.
  • To send an automatic reply when the shop is closed, so you know how to reach us.
  • To keep the business records we are required to keep.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not share your information with other companies for their marketing.

Who else handles it

Your information is stored in the systems we use to run the shop: our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Supabase), our call-tracking provider (CallTrackingMetrics), and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) for messages sent through their apps. We may also share information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it to serve you and to meet our legal, tax and warranty obligations. After that we delete it.

Security

Only shop staff who need your information can see it, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete your information. See our Data Deletion page: https://www.leetransmissionshop.com/data-deletion.html

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
